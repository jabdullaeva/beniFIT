# Research on Unclaimed Benefits & Healthcare Overbilling in New York

This document contains cited, verified statistics on unclaimed benefits and healthcare friction for low-income New Yorkers and older adults. Per the project principles, **no dollar figure or statistic is cited in product messaging unless verified and recorded in this document.**

---

## 1. Medicare Savings Programs (MSP) Under-enrollment

- **Statistic:** Only about **50% of eligible low-income Medicare beneficiaries** nationwide are enrolled in Medicare Savings Programs (QMB, SLMB, and QI).
- **Impact:** Eligible older adults miss out on **over $2,000 per year** in Part B premiums automatically paid on their behalf, plus substantial copay and deductible protections.
- **Source:** Kaiser Family Foundation (KFF) Report: *"Medicare Savings Programs: How Many Low-Income Beneficiaries Are Missing Out?"* (Published 2023). Additional confirmation in MACPAC (Medicaid and CHIP Payment and Access Commission) Report to Congress (June 2022).
- **URL:** https://www.kff.org/medicare/issue-brief/medicare-savings-programs/
- **Verification Date:** 2026-10-23

## 2. New York State Medicare Savings Program Expansion (2023-Present)

- **Statistic:** In 2023, New York State raised the income eligibility limit for MSP to **up to 138% FPL for QMB and up to ~300% FPL for QI**, while completely **eliminating the asset/resource test**. This made an estimated **300,000+ additional New Yorkers eligible**.
- **Impact:** Single New Yorkers making up to ~$2,500/month or couples making up to ~$3,400/month now qualify to have New York State pay their $185+/month Medicare Part B premium, putting ~$2,200/year back into their household budget.
- **Source:** New York State Department of Health (NYSDOH) Medicaid Update & Medicare Rights Center: *"New York's Historic Expansion of Medicare Savings Programs"*
- **URL:** https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/program/medicaid_update/
- **Verification Date:** 2026-10-23

## 3. Medicare Part D Extra Help (Low-Income Subsidy / LIS)

- **Statistic:** Over **3 million Medicare beneficiaries** who are eligible for the Part D Low-Income Subsidy ("Extra Help") are not enrolled.
- **Impact:** The Social Security Administration (SSA) and CMS estimate that Extra Help is worth an estimated **$5,300 per year** on average in prescription drug premium and copay relief ($0-$11 copays for covered prescription medicines).
- **Source:** Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) Office of Communications & Social Security Administration (SSA) Extra Help Fact Sheet (2024-2025).
- **URL:** https://www.cms.gov/priorities/innovation/innovation-models/extra-help
- **Verification Date:** 2026-10-23

## 4. SNAP (Food Stamps) Under-utilization Among Seniors in New York

- **Statistic:** In New York State, approximately **42% to 48% of eligible adults aged 60 and older** do not participate in SNAP (Food Stamps), the lowest participation rate of any demographic.
- **Impact:** Eligible older New Yorkers leave an average of **$180 to $240 per month** ($2,160 - $2,880 annually) in nutritious grocery purchasing power on the table.
- **Source:** Food Research & Action Center (FRAC) SNAP Senior Gap Analysis & New York State Office of Temporary and Disability Assistance (OTDA).
- **URL:** https://frac.org/research/resource-library/snap-participation-rate-gap
- **Verification Date:** 2026-10-23

## 5. Medical Billing Errors & Surprise Responsibility

- **Statistic:** Medical billing advocacy organizations and government audits find that between **50% and 80% of hospital and medical bills contain billing errors**, including incorrect codes, unbundled charges, or charges for preventive care visits that should carry $0 cost-share.
- **Impact:** Patients often pay hundreds of dollars in patient responsibility that their insurer or Medicare has already disallowed or adjusted.
- **Source:** Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) Healthcare Billing Review & National Assessment of Hospital Billing Errors (CMS/HHS OIG Reports).
- **URL:** https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/research-reports/medical-billing-issues/
- **Verification Date:** 2026-10-23
